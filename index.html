ABOUT ME

Hey, I'm Shivam Vishwakarma, a passionate video editor who loves turning raw footage into engaging visual stories. I specialize in short-form content, including Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and engaging social media videos that capture attention and keep viewers hooked.

With skills in Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, and CapCut, I focus on smooth transitions, precise timing, dynamic effects, color grading, sound mixing, and storytelling to bring every idea to life. I'm always exploring new editing techniques and creative styles to improve my craft and deliver high-quality content.

I believe great editing is more than cutting clips—it's about creating an experience. I'm excited to collaborate with creators, brands, and businesses to transform their ideas into compelling short-form videos that stand out.

Let's create something extraordinary together.
